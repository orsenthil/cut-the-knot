Cut the Knot — Interactive Puzzles

Browser ports of puzzles originally published as Java applets on cut-the-knot.org by Alexander Bogomolny.

Rooster, Hen, Farmer and Wife

A Sam Loyd pursuit puzzle on a checkerboard, with a surprising parity argument hiding behind it. Play it, then look for the math.

Flip Them

Flip any M of N triangles at a time and try to turn them all downward — some combinations are impossible, and a simple invariant proves it.

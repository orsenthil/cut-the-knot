Privacy

The short version. You can play everything here without telling us who you are. We show no adverts and we do not track you around the web.

If you sign in, we keep a record of the games you play so you can see it on any device. Only you can see it. You can download it or delete it whenever you like.

If you just play

Nothing about you is sent to us. The site remembers which games you have played, how they went and how long you spent, and it keeps this inside your own browser. Nobody else can read it, and it never leaves your device.

Your browser also remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme.

To wipe it, press Forget my record on your profile, or clear this site's data in your browser.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. Please ask a grown-up first if you are a child. When you sign in with Google or GitHub, we keep in your coolmaths account:

  • the name on your Google or GitHub account, and which of the two you used
  • which games you have played, how many times, your wins and losses, and the time you spent
  • a list of your recent plays, with when each one started and ended
  • the game you have open and where the pieces are, for the games that save this
  • your settings on the profile page

Google or GitHub tell us your email address when you sign in. It is held by the sign-in service so that it knows you next time. We do not use it to write to you, and we do not put it in your record.

We do not keep your password; we never see it. We do not keep your picture.

Who can see your record

You, when you are signed in. The database is locked so that each player can read and change only their own record.

The person who runs this site can open the database in order to keep it working.

Nobody else. There are no public profiles and no leaderboards.

The tutor

coolmaths has a tutor that AI assistants such as Claude can connect to. It cannot see your record unless you connect it: you are shown who is asking and you press Allow.

After that, the assistant you connected can read what you have played and the game you have open. It cannot change anything. What that assistant does with what it reads is up to the company that makes it, so check their privacy page too.

You can stop it at any time: untick Let the coolmaths tutor see my record on your profile.

Other companies involved

We do not sell or share your record. A few companies provide the machinery the site runs on, and so handle some data for us:

  • GitHub serves these pages. Like any web host, it sees your internet address when your browser asks for a page.
  • Google (Firebase) provides sign-in and stores the accounts and records. Its code is only loaded on the pages where you can sign in, and everywhere once you have signed in.
  • Fly.io runs the tutor.
  • Wikipedia: when you point at an underlined maths word, your browser asks Wikipedia for a short summary to show.

There is no advertising and no analytics on this site.

Getting a copy, or deleting it

On your profile:

  • Download my record saves everything we keep about your play as a file.
  • Forget my record deletes what you have played and keeps your account.
  • Delete my account deletes your record, your settings and your sign-in. It cannot be undone.

Questions

The site's code is public. You can read exactly what it does, or ask a question, at github.com/orsenthil/cut-the-knot.
